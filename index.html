AgeNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

AgeNest ("the app") lets you save family members' and friends' names, birth dates, anniversaries and other yearly events, relations, groups and optional photos so it can show their ages, countdowns and reminders, and make greeting cards.

Your family list stays on your device

The names, dates, relations, groups and photos you enter are stored only on your device. AgeNest has no account system, and nothing you enter is ever sent to us or to any third party.

App usage and crash reports

To keep the app working well, AgeNest uses these Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics records how the app is used, for example that a person was added or a photo was cropped, along with device model, OS version, app version, approximate region (from IP address) and a random app-instance identifier. It never includes the names, dates or photos you enter.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report when the app crashes: the technical details of the error, device model, OS version and app version.
  • Firebase Remote Config downloads app settings, such as update and maintenance notices. Google receives a random app-instance identifier to do this.

Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy and security terms. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

You can turn off usage and crash reports at any time in Settings → Privacy → Share usage & crash reports. Remote Config notices keep working when they are off.

Photos

If you pick a photo for a person, it goes through the Android photo picker. The app saves a small copy inside its own private storage. It cannot see any of your other photos.

Greeting cards

Cards you make are drawn on your device. Save puts the image in your phone's Pictures/AgeNest folder; Share hands it to the app you pick (for example a messaging app), which then handles it under its own privacy policy.

Contacts

If you choose Import from contacts, AgeNest asks for permission to read your contacts. It reads names, birthdays and photos on your phone to show you a list, and saves only the people you pick into its own private storage. Contacts are never sent anywhere, and nothing is read until you allow it. You can withdraw the permission at any time in Android's app settings.

Calendar

Add to calendar opens your calendar app with the person's name and birthday filled in. Nothing is saved until you confirm it there, and what happens next (for example syncing to your calendar account) is handled by that app. Export to calendar file writes a file with everyone's name and birthday to the location you choose. AgeNest does not read your calendar.

Backups

When you export a backup, AgeNest writes a file with your family list and the photos you added to the location you choose. What happens to that file after that is up to you. Android's built-in device backup may also include the app's data in your personal Google backup, under your Google account settings.

Notifications

Birthday reminders are scheduled and shown on your device. No data is sent anywhere to deliver them.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes what data is collected (for example by adding ads), we will update this page and the app's Play Store listing before that version is released.
